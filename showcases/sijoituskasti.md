## The opportunity I pitched

Podcast interviews already contain stories that news publishers can turn into articles. I pitched Sijoituskästi a way to publish those stories themselves: adapt existing episodes into readable, sourced articles on their own site, alongside the original podcast.

The selling point was to get more value from content they had already produced. Publishing their own written coverage could bring readers directly to Sijoituskästi, create additional advertising inventory, and make their interviews easier to discover through search engines and AI search. It would also give them the opportunity to publish their perspective before outside outlets cover an episode.

These are proposed benefits, not measured traffic, ranking or revenue results. Publishing an article does not guarantee visibility or citations in AI search.

## What I built

A Finnish article prototype based on Sijoituskästi’s interview with Takomo Golf founder Sebastian Haapahovi. The implementation brings together:

- A narrative opening, concise sections and selective bold highlights for scanning.
- Company facts, attributed quotations and timestamp links back to the episode.
- A clickable YouTube preview and a Spotify listening option.
- Responsive typography, compact layouts and social sharing controls.
- Search and sharing metadata, including structured article data.

The article was checked against the available transcript and supporting company/investor sources. Unverifiable claims were removed during the editorial review.

## The design idea

Help readers understand the story quickly, then make it easy to move between the written article and the full conversation. The article should stand on its own as a useful piece of editorial content, rather than act as a transcript dump.
