## What the site communicates

I help creators get more from their content through video production, livestream content management and editing. The portfolio presents two ways to work with me: an ongoing partnership turning streams into clips and full videos, or dedicated editing for individual projects.

## Design decisions

- Put the services and their supporting results close together so visitors can understand the work quickly.
- Group related metrics instead of presenting a wall of unrelated numbers.
- Use a compact blue and white palette, clear typography and consistent alignment.
- Keep detailed channel figures in an expandable breakdown.
- Use one clear email contact action.

The growth graphic in the site prototype is explicitly illustrative. It describes the wider creator partnership and does not attribute all main-channel growth to my work alone.
